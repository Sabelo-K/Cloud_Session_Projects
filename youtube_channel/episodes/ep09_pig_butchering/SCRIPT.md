## SCENE 1: Cold open hook

**VISUAL:** Penny's phone buzzes. A text from an unknown number: "Hey, are we still on for dinner tonight?" Penny types "Sorry, wrong number!"

**VO:**
> A text arrives from a number Penny doesn't know. / Hey, are we still on for dinner tonight? / She replies, / sorry, wrong number. / That polite reply / is the first step of a scam / that took more than five billion dollars from Americans in a single year.

## SCENE 2: Promise

**VISUAL:** Title card: MONEY MECHANICS. A piggy bank on a conveyor belt heads toward a machine with three dials: "THE FRIEND", "THE FAKE APP" and "THE EXIT FEE".

**VO:**
> Last episode, being broke was expensive. / This time, the target has savings. / It's called pig butchering, / from a Chinese phrase, sha zhu pan. / First the pig is fattened. / Then it's butchered. / Here are the three stages of the machine.

## SCENE 3: Engine 1, the friend

**VISUAL:** Header "ENGINE #1: THE FRIEND". A calendar flips through weeks. Chat bubbles pile up: good morning, photos of fancy dinners, "how was your day?"

**VO:**
> Stage one: / the friend. / The stranger apologises, / and keeps chatting. / Good morning messages. / Photos of a nice life. / Questions about Penny's day, / her job, / her savings. / This can go on for weeks, / sometimes months. / Nobody asks for money. / That's the point. / The scam isn't selling a product. / It's building trust.

**VISUAL:** The friend's photo: a sports car and a beach. A speech bubble: "I made it all trading crypto. My uncle shows me when to buy."

**VO:**
> Then, almost by accident, / the new friend mentions / how they afford that life. / Crypto trading. / An uncle with inside tips. / An app most people haven't heard of. / Penny isn't pushed. / She's invited. / And by now, / the friend knows exactly how much she has, / and even which relatives / might lend her more. / Every chat was also / an interview.

---

## SCENE 4: Engine 2, the fake app

**VISUAL:** Header "ENGINE #2: THE FAKE APP". Penny's phone shows a sleek trading app. She deposits $500. A green line climbs. Balance: $650.

**VO:**
> Stage two: / the fake app. / Penny starts small. / Five hundred dollars. / The app looks real, / with charts, / a support chat, / and a balance that goes up. / Fast.

**VISUAL:** A cutaway reveals the app is a cardboard front. Behind it, a scammer types numbers into a spreadsheet. The real money flows out a pipe labelled "OFFSHORE".

**VO:**
> But the numbers are just numbers. / The money left the moment she sent it. / The profits on screen / are typed in by the scammers. / Sometimes they even let her withdraw a little, / to prove it works. / So Penny sends more. / Then her savings. / Then maybe a loan. / If she hesitates, / the friend is hurt. / Don't you trust me? / The pressure is personal, / not financial, / and that's much harder to say no to.

**VISUAL:** Stat card: "Crypto investment fraud, US, 2024: $5.8 BILLION" (FBI).

**VO:**
> In twenty twenty-four, / the FBI recorded five point eight billion dollars / lost to crypto investment fraud, / the biggest share of all crypto scam losses. / When the FBI started warning victims directly, / about three in four / didn't know they were being scammed.

## SCENE 5: Engine 3, the exit fee

**VISUAL:** Header "ENGINE #3: THE EXIT FEE". Penny taps WITHDRAW. A pop-up: "Withdrawal blocked. Pay 20% tax first."

**VO:**
> Stage three: / the exit fee. / When Penny tries to take her money out, / there's a problem. / A tax to pay first. / A fee to unlock the account. / Each payment / unlocks another fee. / That's the butchering. / The goal is to take / every last dollar, / and when there's nothing left, / the friend disappears.

## SCENE 7: The machine (summary + rules)

**VISUAL:** The three gears turn together: THE FRIEND, THE FAKE APP, THE EXIT FEE.

**VO:**
> So here's the machine. / The friend builds trust. / The fake app shows profits that don't exist. / And the exit fee takes whatever is left. / Three rules to stay out of it. / One: / don't reply to wrong-number texts. / Just delete them. / Two: / never invest through someone you've only met online, / however long you've known them. / Three: / if you have to pay to get your own money out, / it's already gone. / Stop paying, / and report it to the FBI at I C 3 dot gov.
