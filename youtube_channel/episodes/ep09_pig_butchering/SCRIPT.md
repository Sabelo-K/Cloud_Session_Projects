## SCENE 3: Day 1, the wrong number

**VISUAL:** "DAY 1". Two phones: yours and Marcus's, same bubble "Hi Anna, still on for golf Saturday?". Marcus replies "Sorry, wrong number!"; yours stays unanswered. MECHANISM #1: THE FILTER.

> Day one. / Ten past seven on a Tuesday morning. / A text from a number you don't know. / Hi Anna, are we still on for golf on Saturday? / You're not Anna. / Marcus, two desks over, gets one too. / Different name, same message. / He replies, / sorry, wrong number. / You don't reply at all. / You don't delete it either. / The wrong number isn't a mistake. / It's a filter. / Anyone polite enough to answer / has just become a lead.

## SCENE 4: Day 12, the friend

**VISUAL:** "DAY 12". Stack of "good morning" bubbles on Marcus's phone. Chat jumps to a new app. MECHANISM #2: SELL A PERSON.

> Day twelve. / Marcus shows you his phone at lunch. / Her name is Grace. / She texts him good morning at six fifteen, every single day. / She remembers his sister's name, / and the job he hates. / She's never asked for money. / Not once. / Then, almost by accident, / she mentions that her uncle taught her to trade crypto. / And she asks to move the chat to another app. / You notice that. / Fewer people watching. / Here's the mechanism. / The scammer doesn't sell an investment. / They sell a person. / The trust comes first. / The money comes later.

## SCENE 5: Day 30, the stage set

**VISUAL:** "DAY 30". Slick app: candle chart, support chat. $500 counts to $640; $100 withdrawal lands with a ding. MECHANISM #3: THE STAGE SET.

> Day thirty. / Grace sends Marcus a link to an app. / It looks real. / Candle charts, / a support chat, / a logo. / He puts in five hundred dollars. / By Friday, it says six hundred and forty. / He withdraws a hundred, / just to test it, / and it lands in his bank account. / He's convinced. / You're not. / Because that hundred dollars is the bait. / The app is a stage set. / The numbers on the screen are whatever the scammers type. / Letting him take out a little / is how they buy the big deposit.

## SCENE 6: Day 60, the fattening

**VISUAL:** "DAY 60". Coins pour into the app: SAVINGS, RETIREMENT, CAR LOAN; deposited $120,000. Lunch table with an empty chair.

> Day sixty. / Marcus moves his savings. / Then his retirement account. / Then a loan against the Corolla he'd only just paid off. / One hundred and twenty thousand dollars in. / He stops coming to lunch. / For three weeks, you eat across from his empty chair. / You ask him why. / He says he's busy. / That silence is part of the design. / The scam works best / when nobody else is looking.
