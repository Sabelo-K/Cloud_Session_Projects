## Why the hits hit
1. **The viewer wins, quietly.** Every top video is a secret-status fantasy: you are richer, freer or smarter than the people around you, and *nobody knows*. Grievance and victim framings sit at the bottom of the list.
2. **The title formula:** `POV: You <did the enviable thing> — <Nobody Knows / Then Everything Changed>`. It's second person, with an em dash before the twist. "Nobody Knows" appears in 3 of the top 4.
3. **The thumbnail formula:** the same protagonist every time (a calm man in a sage quarter-zip) with a red **"YOU"** and an arrow, set against people showing off (BMWs, paperwork, a crowd). There's almost no headline text. The contrast tells the story.
4. **The cold open is the climax.** For example: "It's 4:52 on a Friday afternoon in July. The thermometer says 98°." That's sensory, present-tense detail. It reaches the payoff moment, then asks "**So, how did you get here?**"
5. **Exact numbers, constantly:** $296,814, $683, $38.75 an hour. Specific figures feel real and get flashed on screen.
6. **Chapters as a ladder:** "Level one… Level two…", each with a time stamp (age, year, day).
7. **A foil character** (Daniel: likeable, overtime-dependent, a $689/month truck) makes the protagonist's choices legible without lecturing.
8. **Plant and payoff.** A line planted early ("The ones who get treated right are the ones who don't need to be here") pays off at the climax.
9. **The look:** AI-illustrated stills in clean line art and muted flat colour, slow push-ins, burned-in captions, soft music. It's cheap to make every day.
10. **Volume and length:** a 20–35 minute video every day builds watch time and suggested-video reach. A lot of their scale comes from this alone.

## What Ep 9 borrowed, and what it didn't
| Borrowed | How |
|---|---|
| A "you win" POV | You recognise the scam; Marcus (the foil) takes the bait; you stop him paying the $27,600 |
| A climax cold open plus "so how did you get here" | 11:48 PM Thursday, then "Because ninety days ago, you got the same text" |
| A day-stamped ladder | DAY 1, 12, 30, 60, 90 stamps |
| Exact numbers on screen | $184,000, $27,600, $500, $640, $100, $120,000 |
| Plant and payoff | "Fewer people watching" and Marcus going quiet, paid off with "The scam works best when nobody else is looking" and "be that person" |
| The "YOU" arrow | Shown in the hook, the climax, the payoff and the thumbnail |
| Their title shape | "POV: … — You Knew Exactly What It Was" |

**Not copied:** their length (we stay at about 5 minutes), their daily cadence, and their AI-image style (we keep the house hand-drawn Penny look so the channel stays recognisable).

**Honest caveat:** nobody can guarantee a video goes viral. These choices line the episode up with what's measurably working for this channel, but a lot of POV Finance's reach comes from daily 25-minute uploads to a 25K-subscriber audience. A POV series would need several episodes before we can judge it fairly.
