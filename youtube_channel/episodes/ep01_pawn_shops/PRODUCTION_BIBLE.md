# Ep. 1 Production Bible: Shot List, Assets, Thumbnail

Style: flat 2D, thick outlines (4px at 1080p), limited animation (12fps on twos for characters, 24fps for UI and number pops).
Canvas: 1920×1080, 24fps. Keep important elements away from the frame edges.

## Asset list (build once, reuse across the series)
**Reusable across episodes** (the library that makes 2 videos a week sustainable):
- **Penny** turnaround (front, ¾, side) plus poses: idle, walking, worried, shocked (jaw drop), confident, thinking, waving. Mouth chart with 6 shapes for lip-flap. Eyes: blink, side-glance.
- **Generic shopkeeper** (here, the pawnbroker): idle, smirk-to-camera, handing over cash, inspecting with magnifier.
- **UI kit:** number counter, bar chart, calendar flip, checklist with tick, stamp, price tag, "APR" badge, phone bank-app screen.
- **Money props:** cash stack (thin and thick), coin, coin jar (fill states 0–4), credit card (glowing variant).
- **Title card:** MONEY MECHANICS logo sting (≤ 2s), with gears turning.

**Episode-specific:**
- Pawn shop interior BG (counter, shelves of guitars/TVs/jewellery, neon "PAWN" sign).
- Guitar, gold ring, digital scale, car with check-engine light, repair bill.
- "Pawn machine" gears (LOANS / FEES / RESALE).
- US map with store dots, skyscraper with stock ticker.

## Thumbnail
**Concept A (primary):** Penny on the left, shocked and clutching the guitar. On the right, a huge pawnbroker hand offers a tiny cash stack. Big text top-right: **"$150?!"** in yellow with a black outline. Background split yellow/ink.
**Concept B:** Coin jar overflowing with a pawn ticket on the front. Text: **"THE TRAP"**.
**Concept C:** Pawn shop neon sign, with "PAWN" crossed out and "FEES" written over it.

Rules: at most 3 words, readable at phone size (test at 160px wide), one face with a strong expression, and don't repeat the title text.
