## Sources (fact-checked 2026-10-19)
1. **Gift cards:** Bankrate survey: 47% of US adults hold at least one unused gift card, voucher or store credit, averaging $187, about $23B in total. https://www.ksat.com/business/2023/12/25/the-secret-life-of-gift-cards-heres-what-happens-to-the-billions-that-go-unspent-each-year/
2. **Gyms:** DellaVigna & Malmendier, "Paying Not to Go to the Gym" (American Economic Review, 2006): members paying over $70 a month averaged 4.8 visits a month, more than $17 a visit against a $10 per-visit option; about 80% would have saved paying per visit. https://nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w10819/w10819.pdf. Planet Fitness averages about 6,500 members per gym that holds roughly 300 (NPR Planet Money): https://www.npr.org/sections/money/2014/12/30/373996649/why-we-sign-up-for-gym-memberships
3. **Printer ink:** Which? (Sept 2022): Epson ink at £1.78 per ml against Dom Pérignon at 24p per ml, over 7 times more; third-party ink saves over £180 a year. https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/which-calls-for-action-over-printer-inks-costing-up-to-seven-times-more-than-dom-perignon-champagne-aeHrW6g7B3vb
4. **Extended warranties:** Abito & Salant, Review of Economic Studies (2018), via Kellogg Insight: warranties averaged 24% of product price; TVs failed 5–8% of the time; buyers acted as if the risk were 13%; Businessweek reported 50–60% margins. https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/article/extended-warranty-worth-it
5. **Airline miles:** Delta earned about $8.2B from its American Express partnership in 2025 and targets about $10B a year; Jefferies estimates Delta's and United's programs could each be worth $30B+. https://www.itiger.com/news/2659365904
