Penny buys a new TV.
At the till: want to add an extended warranty?
In one study, warranties cost about a quarter of the product's price.
But TVs in that study failed only five to eight percent of the time.
Researchers found buyers acted like the risk was thirteen percent.
That gap is the profit.
Margins on these warranties were estimated at over fifty percent.
The fear sells the warranty. The math keeps the money.
