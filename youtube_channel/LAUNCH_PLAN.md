# Faceless Animated Finance Channel: Launch Plan

**Format:** hand-animated long-form videos, about 5 minutes each, uploaded twice a week.
**Channel:** Money Mechanics (AI voiceover).
**Niche:** animated money explainers. The core series is "How X Actually Makes Money".
**Goal:** reach YPP in about 60 days.
**Keyword data:** vidIQ, pulled 2026-10-12/13. Search volumes are estimates.

---

## 1. Watch-hour math for 5-minute videos

At 5 minutes per video, a viewer typically watches about 50%, which is **~2.5 minutes per view**.

| Target | Hours needed | Views needed (at 2.5 min avg) |
|---|---|---|
| Early YPP (500 subs, 3,000 hrs) | 3,000 | **~72,000** |
| Full YPP (1,000 subs, 4,000 hrs) | 4,000 | **~96,000** |

Plan for about 17 videos by day 60, which works out to an average of about 5,600 views per video. New channels rarely average that, so realistically **one or two videos have to break out**. That shapes the plan:
- **Retention is the main metric to optimise.** Every point of average view duration (AVD) lowers the number of views we need.
- Pick topics with **proven search demand** (below) so videos keep getting found long after upload.
- Link every video to the next one with end screens and a "watch next" line, so one viewer session counts as several views.
- Run a weekly review loop (section 7).
- **Fallback:** if we're under about 50% of the target at day 45, add one 8–10 minute "deep dive" a week. Longer videos earn about 2x the watch hours per view.

## 2. Channel identity (shortlist)

Before choosing, check that the handle is free on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

| Name | Feel |
|---|---|
| **Money Mechanics** | clear, says what we do, works for search |
| **The Fine Print** | hints at hidden tricks, a natural fit for scam and legal episodes |
| **Cash Logic** | short and brandable |
| **Behind the Price Tag** | strong fit for the "how companies make money" series |

**Mascot:** one recurring character, e.g. "Penny", an anxious everyday person who keeps getting outplayed by the system. The audience learns alongside them.
**Style:** flat 2D in a limited palette (2 brand colours plus neutrals) with thick outlines. Use **limited animation**: a library of reusable poses, mouth flaps and backgrounds, and animate only the parts that matter. This is what makes two videos a week sustainable.

## 3. The 5-minute video template (about 750 words at 150 wpm)

| Time | Beat | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Cold open hook.** A surprising claim or number ("Costco loses money on your hot dog… on purpose.") | Keep the 30-second retention above 70% |
| 0:20–0:40 | **Promise.** What they'll understand by the end | Set up the payoff |
| 0:40–1:30 | **Setup.** Penny's situation and the "obvious" explanation | Relatability |
| 1:30–3:30 | **The mechanism.** 2–3 reveals, each with a visual metaphor and one number | The core content; a re-hook every ~45s |
| 3:30–4:30 | **The twist / so-what.** How it affects the viewer's own money | Personal stakes |
| 4:30–5:00 | **Payoff and bridge.** One-line takeaway, then tease the next video | Session time |

Rules: no channel intro, no "like and subscribe" in the first minute, and no false claims. Cite sources in the description, both for viewer trust and for the inauthentic-content policy.

## 5. Production pipeline (per video, target ≤ 3 days)

1. **Research and script** (half a day): 750 words, 3+ sources, a hook in the first sentence.
2. **Voiceover** (1 hr): a consistent human voice, or one licensed AI voice used the same way every time, plus a human-edited script.
3. **Animatic** (2 hrs): rough storyboard timed to the VO. Most of the pacing fixes happen here.
4. **Animation** (1–1.5 days): reuse the asset library and add new assets only where they serve a reveal.
5. **Sound and edit** (2 hrs): music bed, SFX on every reveal, captions.
6. **Packaging** (1 hr): 3 thumbnail concepts and 3 titles, then pick the best.

**$0 toolkit (no subscriptions):**
| Job | Free tool |
|---|---|
| Drawing and frame animation | **Krita** (has an animation timeline) or **OpenToonz** (the free studio tool used on Studio Ghibli productions) |
| Rigged / cut-out animation (fastest for reusable poses) | **Blender** (Grease Pencil) or **Synfig** |
| Editing, captions, audio mix | **DaVinci Resolve** (free version) |
| Voiceover | **Kokoro TTS** (open-source, Apache-2.0, free for commercial use, runs on your own computer). Ep. 1 already has a voiceover from vidIQ bonus credits |
| Music and SFX | **YouTube Audio Library** (free, cleared for monetization) |
| Thumbnails | **Canva free** plan, or Krita / GIMP / Photopea |
| Research and keywords | YouTube search autocomplete, Google Trends, vidIQ free tier |

Hardware: a mouse works for flat limited-animation styles. A basic drawing tablet helps but isn't required.

**Buffer rule:** have 5 finished videos before launch, and never let the buffer drop below 2.

---

## 7. Weekly KPI review

| Metric | Healthy target |
|---|---|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | ≥ 5% |
| 30-second retention | ≥ 70% |
| Average view duration (AVD) | ≥ 50% (~2.5 min) |
| Returning viewers | rising week over week |
| Subs per 1K views | ≥ 5 |

If CTR is low, fix the title or thumbnail; you can swap them on live videos. If retention is low, fix the hook and pacing in the next script.

---

## 8. Policy guardrails

- Every script is original and researched. No reused clips and no templated, repetitive videos.
- Mark any synthetic voice or imagery as altered or synthetic content where it applies.
- Educational framing, not financial advice. Add a disclaimer in the description.
- Use licensed music and fonts only.
