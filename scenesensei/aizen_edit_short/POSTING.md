## Why it's built this way (from researching top Bleach Shorts)
The best-performing Bleach Shorts are Aizen edits: hard cuts synced to phonk, "Aizen tricks the Almighty" story beats, and memes like *"Since when were you under the impression…"*. This Short copies that formula:
- **0–1s hook:** a question on screen ("HOW AIZEN FOOLED A GOD") plus a paradox in the voiceover (*"could see every future… still got played by a man chained to a chair"*).
- **A new visual every 1.5–3s**, with camera shake, zoom punches, color-split glitches and flashes on every cut and beat.
- **Word-by-word captions** with key words in yellow, because most Shorts are watched muted at first.
- **The bass drop lands on "Sosuke Aizen"** (~20s), the moment of highest energy, just as retention usually starts to dip.
- **Meme payoff ending, then a comment prompt**, and it fades to black on a closed-eye opening so it loops cleanly.
